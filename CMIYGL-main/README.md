## Extras
- **Signature styling** – pick a font, ink colour and size (✎ Signature, bottom of the screen). Applies to typed and drawn signatures and is remembered between visits.
- **More card colours** – 7 extra tints plus a custom colour picker, on top of the original 4.
- **Result actions** – Copy image, Share (mobile), Save as JPG.
- **Esc** closes any open popup.

Extras live in `static/js/app.js` and `static/css/extras.css`; `main.*.chunk.js` has small hooks that call into them.
